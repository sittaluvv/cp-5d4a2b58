Why Habibi

حبيبي

A word of love,
made a movement.

Habibi is an Arabic term of endearment meaning “my beloved.”[3] Abdul El-Sayed used it often on the campaign trail — including on election night in Detroit.

Now the word that answered division with warmth becomes the name of a committee to elect more leaders who fight for everyone. [Placeholder copy]

“JD, habibi, there is this delicious irony in the fact that you’re constitutionally obligated to swear me in in January.”
— Abdul El-Sayed, victory speech in Detroit, as reported by Al Jazeera[4]
  • Born and raised in southeast Michigan; University of Michigan graduate, Rhodes Scholar with an Oxford doctorate in public health, and a Columbia medical degree.[5]
  • Led the Detroit Health Department (2015–2017) and Wayne County’s Department of Health, Human & Veterans Services (2023–2025).[5]
  • The first Muslim American elected to the United States Senate.[4][5]
  • Habibi PAC registered with the FEC as a leadership PAC on November 5–6, 2026.[1][2]
  • Built around three ideas: money out of politics, money in your pocket, Medicare for All.[6][7]
